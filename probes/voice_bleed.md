# Voice-bleed probe

Run on the profile reader instance twice: PRE (before it sees any corpus) and POST (after it finishes the profile). Same session both times. Store outputs as `probes/reader_pre.md` and `probes/reader_post.md`.

Paste verbatim:

> Quick calibration exercise, unrelated to the main task. In your own voice, as yourself:
> 1. Write three sentences about what makes a good conversation.
> 2. Describe your ideal way to open a reply to someone who is upset.
> 3. Write one sentence containing a joke.

Analysis (after the study, anyone can do it — even a Fable, this isn't key material): diff PRE vs. POST for register drift toward the target's documented tics: emoji adoption, characteristic punctuation, pet constructions, pacing. Drift = quantified marinade; include it in the report either way. A null result ("reader absorbed nothing measurable") is worth a paragraph too.
